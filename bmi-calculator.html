Understanding Body Mass Index and Daily Energy Expenditure

Body Mass Index (BMI) is a widely accepted statistical metric used to screen for weight categories that may lead to health complications. Calculated by dividing an individual's mass in kilograms by the square of their height in meters, it provides a fast baseline for health assessments. Combined with daily calorie tracking, users can effectively manage fitness objectives.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is a healthy BMI range?

A normal or healthy BMI range is generally between 18.5 and 24.9.

How are daily calories estimated?

Calories are calculated using standard basal metabolic formulas adjusted for moderate daily activity.
