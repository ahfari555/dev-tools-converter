The Power of Exponential Growth: Understanding Compound Interest

Often termed the "eighth wonder of the world," compound interest is the financial phenomenon where investment earnings generate their own returns over subsequent periods. Unlike simple interest—which calculates yields solely on the original principal—compounding accelerates wealth accumulation exponentially across time horizons.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is compound interest?

Interest calculated on both initial principal and accumulated interest from prior periods.

Why is time horizon critical?

Longer time frames allow exponential growth curves to compound earnings dramatically.
