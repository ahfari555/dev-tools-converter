Reference Dictionary

Technical & Financial Glossary

Explore official definitions and standardized metrics used across our commercial, scientific, and health calculation tools.

1. Sales Tax & VAT (Value Added Tax)

Sales Tax is a consumption tax imposed by government authorities on the sale of goods and services at the point of retail purchase. VAT (Value Added Tax) is an incremental consumption tax assessed on the value added to goods at each stage of production and distribution supply chains.

2. Absolute Zero & Kelvin

Absolute Zero (0 K) represents the lowest theoretical thermodynamic temperature state at which particles possess minimal vibrational motion. The Kelvin (K) scale is the absolute thermodynamic temperature scale where zero marks absolute zero.

3. Avoirdupois Weight System

The Avoirdupois System is a measurement system of mass based on a pound containing 16 ounces. It serves as the customary standard for retail commerce and freight logistics across the United States.

4. Body Mass Index (BMI)

BMI is a statistical screening tool that estimates human body fat based on an individual's mass relative to height, calculated as weight in kilograms divided by height in meters squared ($kg/m^2$).

5. Loan Amortization & EMI

Amortization is the scheduled process of spreading out loan payments over time into fixed installments. EMI (Equated Monthly Installment) represents the fixed periodic payment made by a borrower to a lender to clear both principal and interest.
