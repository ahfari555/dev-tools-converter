Master Technical & Financial Glossary (A–Z)

Browse our extensive database of technical definitions, financial metrics, scientific units, and health parameters.

Absolute Zero

The lowest theoretical thermodynamic temperature state (0 Kelvin / -273.15°C) where all thermal molecular motion stops.

Amortization

The scheduled financial process of spreading out loan repayments into regular fixed periodic installments over time.

Avoirdupois Weight System

The standard Imperial measurement system of mass based on a pound comprising exactly 16 ounces.

Basal Metabolic Rate (BMR)

The total amount of energy (calories) expended by a human body at complete rest in a neutrally temperate environment.

Body Mass Index (BMI)

A standard statistical screening metric evaluating human body fat based on mass relative to squared height.

Celsius Scale (°C)

A metric temperature scale where water freezes at 0°C and boils at 100°C under standard atmospheric pressure.

Credit Hour (Academic)

A quantitative unit used in higher education institutions to measure the instructional time assigned to a course.

Equated Monthly Installment (EMI)

The fixed monetary amount paid by a borrower to a financial lender each calendar month to clear debt liabilities.

Fahrenheit Scale (°F)

An imperial temperature scale where pure water freezes at 32°F and boils at 212°F across 180 graduated intervals.

Grade Point Average (GPA)

A standardized numerical average representing a student's academic standing weighted by course credit hours.

Kilogram (kg)

The primary base SI unit of mass defined precisely using fundamental quantum physical constants like Planck's constant.

Length Dimension

The physical measurement of spatial extent along one dimension, spanning metric meters to imperial inches.

Markdown (Retail Discount)

A percentage reduction applied directly to a product's original list price during retail promotional campaigns.

Mortgage Principal

The original capital sum borrowed from a lending institution to finance property acquisition before interest charges.

Sales Tax

A statutory consumption tax assessed by government jurisdictions on retail commercial transactions.

Value Added Tax (VAT)

An incremental consumption tax applied to product value additions across commercial supply chain stages.
