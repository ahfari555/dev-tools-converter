2. The 50/30/20 Rule for Personal Finance

One of the most widely recommended budgeting frameworks is the 50/30/20 rule, popularized by financial experts to simplify income distribution:

  • 50% for Needs: Essential living expenses including housing utilities, groceries, health insurance, minimum debt payments, and basic transportation.
  • 30% for Wants: Lifestyle choices such as dining out, entertainment subscriptions, shopping items, and recreational travel.
  • 20% for Savings & Debt: Building an emergency fund, contributing to retirement accounts, and paying down high-interest liabilities aggressively.
