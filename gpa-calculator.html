Understanding Academic Grade Point Averages (GPA)

A Grade Point Average (GPA) serves as the primary quantitative summary of a student's academic performance in secondary or higher education institutions. By weighting course credit hours against letter grade numerical equivalents, academic advisors and prospective employers evaluate scholarly diligence consistently.

Frequently Asked Questions

How is GPA calculated?

Divide total quality points earned by total credit hours attempted.

What constitutes a high GPA?

A GPA of 3.5 or higher generally qualifies students for academic honors.
